# Textures, patterns and surfaces

## Included in this pack

### Generated overlays

- `assets/textures/overlays/vignette.png` — transparent edge darkening.

The full local edition adds:

- 7 Poly Haven CC0 photo tiles: concrete, brushed concrete, plaster, stone
  tiles, fabric, metal plate, wood.
- 7 procedural tiles: grain fine/soft/coarse, paper ivory/white/kraft, brushed
  metal.
- 10 alpha overlays: bokeh, dust/scratches, film burn, halftone, three light
  leaks, noise, topographic lines, vignette.

### Patterns

`assets/patterns/` — 13 transparent SVGs: dots, dots-grid, grid, grid-fine,
diagonal, crosshatch, chevron, waves, hex, triangles, plus, halftone, rings.
Recolor the `#fff` fills or use them as alpha masks.

## Using textures without making a mess

- **Scale**: a texture at 100% on 4K is visible; scale it 4x and it becomes
  blotches. Match the size to the final resolution.
- **Opacity**: 3-12% for grain; 8-25% for surfaces; 30-60% for a clearly
  material look.
- **Blend mode**: `soft-light` for grain, `overlay` for contrast, `multiply` to
  darken, `screen` for light.
- **Tile**: check seams by repeating 2x2 and looking at the center.
- **Color**: tint the texture with the dominant color of the piece; do not mix
  temperatures without intent.
- **Unification**: a very soft grain layer over the whole collage makes
  disparate elements feel like one piece.

## Recipes

The reduced pack ships only the vignette overlay. The paper, grain and light
leak commands below require the full local texture set or generated/fetched
equivalents.

```bash
# soft grain on an image
magick base.png \( assets/textures/tiles/procedural/grain-soft.png \
  -resize 3840x2160^ -gravity center -extent 3840x2160 \) \
  -compose soft-light -composite out.png

# paper background
magick assets/textures/tiles/procedural/paper-ivory.jpg \
  -resize 3840x2160^ -gravity center -extent 3840x2160 bg.jpg

# tint an SVG pattern
sed 's/#fff/#89b4fa/g' assets/patterns/dots.svg > /tmp/dots-blue.svg
rsvg-convert -w 3840 -h 2160 /tmp/dots-blue.svg -o dots-blue.png

# vignette
magick base.jpg \( assets/textures/overlays/vignette.png \
  -resize 3840x2160^ -gravity center -background none -extent 3840x2160 \) \
  -compose over -composite out.jpg

# light leak
magick base.jpg \( assets/textures/overlays/light-leak-warm.png \
  -resize 3840x2160^ -gravity center -background none -extent 3840x2160 \) \
  -compose screen -composite out.jpg
```
