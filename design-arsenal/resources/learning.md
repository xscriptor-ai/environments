## Deliberate practice

1. **Copy a mechanism**, not a piece: reproduce only the structure.
2. **One constraint per study**: only 2 colors, one font, geometry, natural
   light.
3. **Series of 10**: same theme, 10 variations; the tenth is often the good one.
4. **Critique with vocabulary**: focus, hierarchy, rhythm, contrast, scale,
   alignment, density, temperature.
5. **Measure**: contrast, size, weight on disk, load time, legibility at
   distance.
6. **Archive**: save the recipe (command/script) with the result.
7. **Redo**: revisit an old piece with the new tools.
