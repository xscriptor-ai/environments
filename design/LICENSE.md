## Attribution templates

```text
“Title” by Author (Year).
Source: URL
License: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Changes: cropped, extended canvas, color graded, grain added.
```

For CC BY-SA derivatives, state the license of the derivative. For fonts,
preserve the OFL notice when redistributing font files. For model-generated
assets, record the model, checkpoint and terms of use.

This is practical guidance, not legal advice. Verify the license at the
original source before commercial use.
